Odd Alliance Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Odd Alliance is developed by Jared Robinson under the gameplaycomesfirst brand. For support or privacy inquiries, email gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com.

Saved games and preferences

Odd Alliance runs matches locally on your device. It stores saved matches, player names entered for local play, audio preferences, enabled DLC packs and purchase entitlement information in Android's app-private storage. These records let the game resume matches and remember your settings. The game does not upload your match records to a developer-operated server.

Google Play purchases and diagnostics

The game integrates Google Play Billing for optional DLC purchases. Google Play handles its checkout process. The app queries purchased products, receives purchase status and uses purchase tokens to acknowledge completed purchases and restore access. It does not receive your payment card number or store purchase tokens in saved matches.

The integrated Google Play Billing library also sends billing diagnostics through Google's transport services. These messages describe billing responses and operation information, including timing, the app package and version, Android version, and device model/manufacturer and software build information. This diagnostic processing is separate from your local saved matches and audio preferences.

The diagnostic transport can also attach country and language/locale settings, time-zone offset, network/cellular connection type, mobile network operator codes, and app build details.

Google receives and handles checkout information and billing diagnostics through its services, subject to Google's privacy policy. Google's policy describes its processing, security, retention and data controls. You can use Google's account and privacy controls to manage information retained by its services.

Android backup and device transfers

The app permits Android backup. Depending on your device and system settings, Android may back up app data and restore or transfer it to another device. These services are controlled by Android and your backup provider. Review your device's backup settings if you do not want app data included.

Data retention and deletion

Saved games and preferences remain in app storage until replaced or cleared. You can clear local app storage using Android Settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app does not cancel a Google Play purchase or remove Google's purchase records. Backup copies and restored data depend on the system/backup provider; manage those using the provider's controls.

Support and privacy requests

If you email gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com, the support inbox receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Those messages are used to respond to your support or privacy request. The inbox is hosted by Google's Gmail service.

Support emails and their attachments are deleted 90 days after the related issue is resolved. Send privacy questions or requests concerning your correspondence to the support/privacy address above. Google's own handling of email service data is governed by its privacy policy.

Accounts and advertising

Odd Alliance does not require an app-specific account or login. This version does not display third-party advertisements. Google Play billing diagnostics are separate from advertising and from your local match records.

Audience

Odd Alliance is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13.

Security

Local game data is stored in Android's app-private storage. Google's billing diagnostic transport uses HTTPS. Google Play, Android backup and Gmail operate their own services and security controls. Clearing local game data does not delete information retained by those services.
