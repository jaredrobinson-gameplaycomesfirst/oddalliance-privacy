Odd Alliance Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Odd Alliance is developed by Jared Robinson under the gameplaycomesfirst brand. For support or privacy inquiries, email gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com.

Current closed-testing release

In closed-testing version 110 (5.68), all available faction packs are included free and purchases are unavailable. This build does not initialize Google Play Billing, query or acknowledge purchases, or send Billing diagnostics. It opts out of Android cloud backup. Earlier Billing-enabled versions and features that may be enabled in a future launch release are described separately below.

Saved games and preferences

Odd Alliance runs matches locally on your device. It stores saved matches, player names entered for local play, audio preferences, enabled DLC packs and locally saved entitlement information in Android's app-private storage. These records let the game resume matches and remember your settings. The game does not upload your match records to a developer-operated server.

Earlier Billing-enabled versions and possible launch features

Earlier Billing-enabled versions integrate Google Play Billing for optional DLC purchases; a future launch release may enable these features again. In those versions, Google Play handles checkout, and the app queries purchased products, receives purchase status and uses purchase tokens to acknowledge completed purchases and restore access. It does not receive your payment card number or store purchase tokens in saved matches.

In Billing-enabled versions, the Google Play Billing library also sends billing diagnostics through Google's transport services. These messages include a randomly generated client-session identifier, billing responses and operation information, including timing, the app package and version, Android version, and device model/manufacturer and software build information. This diagnostic processing is separate from your local saved matches and audio preferences.

The diagnostic transport can also attach country and language/locale settings, time-zone offset, network/cellular connection type, mobile network operator codes, and app build details.

For Billing-enabled versions, Google receives and handles checkout information and billing diagnostics through its services, subject to Google's privacy policy. Google's policy describes its processing, security, retention and data controls. You can use Google's account and privacy controls to manage information retained by its services.

Android backup and device transfers

Closed-testing version 110 opts out of Android cloud backup and excludes app data from standard Android device-to-device transfers. Local progress is not restored through this build's configured cloud-backup mechanism. Some manufacturers' or privileged migration tools may behave differently, and these settings do not delete older backups. Earlier versions permitted Android backup; a future launch release may enable it again. Backup and restoration in those versions depend on your device, system settings and backup provider.

Data retention and deletion

Saved games and preferences remain in app storage until replaced or cleared. You can clear local app storage using Android Settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app does not cancel an earlier Google Play purchase, remove Google's purchase or Billing diagnostic records, or delete older cloud backups. Manage records retained by those services using their providers' controls.

Support and privacy requests

If you email gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com, the support inbox receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Those messages are used to respond to your support or privacy request. The inbox is hosted by Google's Gmail service.

Support emails and their attachments are deleted 90 days after the related issue is resolved. Send privacy questions or requests concerning your correspondence to the support/privacy address above. Google's own handling of email service data is governed by its privacy policy.

Accounts and advertising

Odd Alliance does not require an app-specific account or login. This closed-testing version does not display third-party advertisements. In Billing-enabled versions, Google Play billing diagnostics are separate from advertising and from your local match records.

Audience

Odd Alliance is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13.

Security

Local game data is stored in Android's app-private storage. In Billing-enabled versions, Google's billing diagnostic transport uses HTTPS. Google Play, Android backup and Gmail operate their own services and security controls. Clearing local game data does not delete information retained by those services.

This policy website

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub privacy statement. This policy page contains no analytics scripts, cookies, forms or external embedded assets. Opening it from the app uses your browser. Following external links or contacting support uses the corresponding provider's services and policies.
