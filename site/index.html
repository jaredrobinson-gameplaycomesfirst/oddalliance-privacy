Odd Alliance Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Odd Alliance is developed by Jared Robinson under the gameplaycomesfirst brand. For support or privacy inquiries, email gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com.

Versions and private testing

Odd Alliance is being developed and tested through Google Play. Version 113 (5.71) uses free, device-local Shop unlocks and offline ad previews. Provider-enabled testing builds use Google Play checkout and Google Mobile Ads demo advertisements as described below. Some earlier versions also enabled Google Play Billing. The Shop identifies the testing mode. Google's license testers can use Google's test payment methods; participation in a testing track alone does not make purchases free.

Saved games and preferences

Matches run locally on your device. The app stores saved matches, player names entered for local play, language and audio preferences, enabled DLC packs, ad-placement completion markers, and entitlement records in Android's app-private storage. Ordinary play does not require a separate game account. In wallet-enabled builds, players who choose Google sign-in send the limited reward records described below to the game's wallet backend; detailed match saves remain local.

Optional Google sign-in and Renown wallet

Wallet-enabled builds offer optional Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication. Google/Firebase processes your Google account identity, including account ID, email address and basic profile information provided during sign-in. The game uses the resulting Firebase user ID to associate your Renown balance and earned DLC with your account. We do not receive your Google password.

The wallet backend uses Google Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions. It stores a generated match identifier, player count, victory-point target, registration and claim timestamps, completion outcome, final score summary, turn/round counts, balance, earned DLC grants and a transaction ledger. Records support reward calculation, duplicate prevention, account restoration and abuse prevention. Detailed card-by-card histories and local player names are not required. Sign-in, collection and redemption require a network connection. You may play ordinary matches without signing in.

Firebase App Check uses Play Integrity to help verify that requests come from the game. Google processes the associated app/device integrity information and installation identifiers. These checks do not prove that an offline match was played honestly. We do not sell Renown, offer cash redemption or transfers, or condition the normal match reward on ad viewing.

Sign-out removes access to earned DLC on that device until you sign back into the same wallet account. Google Play purchases remain separate. A per-account wallet cache and pending reward requests are stored locally for retries. See Firebase privacy information.

Google Play purchases and diagnostics

In Billing-enabled builds, Google Play handles optional DLC, Ad-free and bundle checkout. The app queries product prices and account purchases, receives purchase status, authenticates Google's signed purchase receipts, and sends purchase tokens to Google Play to acknowledge completed purchases and restore access. Signed receipts, including product IDs and tokens, are stored in app-private storage so purchased content can remain available offline. Tokens are not included in match saves or exports. The app does not receive your payment-card number.

The Google Play Billing library also sends billing diagnostics through Google's transport services. These can include a randomly generated client-session identifier, billing responses and operation timings, app package/version, Android version, device model/manufacturer and software build information, country and language settings, time-zone offset, network/cellular connection type and mobile network operator codes. This processing is separate from your local match records.

Google handles checkout, purchase records and billing diagnostics subject to Google's privacy policy. Google controls its services' retention, security and account/privacy controls. Device-local free test unlocks from preview builds are separate from Google Play purchases.

Google advertisements and privacy choices

Ads-enabled builds integrate Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Private provider tests use Google's demo ad units and do not generate paid advertising traffic. Launch builds configured for live advertising use the publisher's ad units and may show revenue-generating advertisements. Demo ads still use Google's SDK and network; they are not offline previews and do not mean that no data is transmitted.

When enabled and permitted by the consent state, Google's ad SDK collects and shares information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This includes IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions such as app launches, taps and views, performance/diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as app set ID and other applicable identifiers. Current private builds remove the Android advertising-ID permission; other SDK data collection can still occur. See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

UMP contacts Google to determine whether a privacy message is required and manages the associated consent/opt-out preferences. Where required, the app presents Google's privacy message and offers Ad privacy options in Settings and the Shop. Ad requests begin only when UMP permits them. If the required consent configuration or an ad is unavailable, the placement is skipped. Google's SDK transmits its data using TLS.

Ads-enabled builds place a banner in Collection and a full-screen ad after an eligible completed match, before results. The app does not place banners on the main menu or during gameplay. Ad-free removes these ad placements. Purchasing Ad-free does not remove Google Play purchase processing or delete information previously received by Google.

Android backup and device transfers

Current private builds opt out of Android cloud backup and exclude app data from standard Android device-to-device transfers. Some manufacturers' or privileged migration tools may behave differently, and these settings do not delete older backups. Earlier versions permitted Android backup. Those backups depend on your device, system settings and backup provider.

Data retention and deletion

Local saves, preferences, consent settings and entitlement records remain in app storage until replaced or cleared. Android Settings lets you clear local app storage or uninstall the app. These actions do not cancel a Google Play purchase or delete Google's purchase, diagnostic or advertising records or older cloud backups. Manage records retained by Google and other providers through their own account/privacy controls. A later installation can query your Google Play account again to restore purchased content.

Support and privacy requests

To delete an online wallet account and its associated balance, earned DLC and transaction records, open Renown Wallet → Delete Wallet Account, confirm and authenticate again. You can also request deletion without the app at the account deletion page. Account data is retained until deletion is requested. Deletion does not cancel Play purchases, delete your Google account or remove records Google independently retains. A hashed deletion marker is kept for seven days to block stale tokens and removed by daily cleanup, normally within eight days. Infrastructure providers may retain operational backups/logs under their own retention policies.

If you email gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com, the support inbox receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Those messages are used to respond to your support or privacy request. The inbox is hosted by Google's Gmail service.

Support emails and their attachments are deleted 90 days after the related issue is resolved. Send privacy questions or requests concerning your correspondence to the support/privacy address above. Google's own handling of email service data is governed by its privacy policy.

Audience

Odd Alliance is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13.

Security

Local game data is stored in Android's app-private storage. Google's billing diagnostic transport uses HTTPS, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK encrypts transmitted data using TLS. Google Play, AdMob, UMP, Android backup and Gmail operate their own services and security controls. Clearing local game data does not delete information retained by those services.
